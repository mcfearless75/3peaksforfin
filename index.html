Why We're Walking

Fin's story

Fin was born with a complex heart condition and needs life-changing heart surgery in America that is not available on the NHS.

Every donation brings Fin closer to a second chance at life.

That's why we're taking on the Yorkshire Three Peaks Challenge — to raise as much as we can towards the cost of Fin's surgery and give him and his family hope for the future. Thank you for reading Fin's story and for anything you're able to give.
